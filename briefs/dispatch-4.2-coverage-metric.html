How we’ll judge Dispatch 4.2

We will judge 4.2 by one number: the share of callouts nobody took, which doubled from 5.5% before the release to 11.1% after, or from about 1 in 18 to 1 in 9.

We read it as a 4-week average with a target of 6.5% or lower, because single weeks swing a lot and the 5.5% on 31 Aug made it look fixed when it wasn’t.

Next to it we track how many responders are locked out (4 today, target 0), because neighbours covering for them can make coverage look fine while the problem spreads.

Split by area, the stuck responders’ own areas are covered, but the neighbours doing the covering now miss pings at home and their areas are still at 12% unanswered, so the fix (back to a 90-second wait plus a one-time score repair) needs to go in the next release.
