Dispatch 4.2 is not healthy

Over the last four weeks, 1 in 9 callouts went unanswered. Before 4.2 it was 1 in 20. Almost all of the increase comes from responders missing pings under the new 60-second wait.

The grey part is the normal level: some callouts always go unanswered because the responder said no. The orange part is new. Before 4.2 it was about 1 callout a month. In the last four weeks it was 26.

What happened

  1. 4.2 cut the time a responder has to answer a ping from 90 seconds to 60.
  2. Responders who answer slowly started timing out. Dispatch scores a timeout the same as a no, and the score never recovers by itself.
  3. Four responders (Vesper, Farlight, The Undertow, Meteor Mite) sank far enough that Dispatch almost stopped pinging them. Neighbours now cover their areas and miss more pings at home.
  4. The weekly take-rate report looks recovered only because those four barely get pinged. Two of their handlers never filed a ticket.

Decisions needed

  1. Approve a slot in the next release to restore the 90-second wait and repair the four responders’ scores. Keep the proximity weights.
  2. Adopt unanswered callouts (4-week) as the health metric for 4.2, reported with the locked-out count, instead of the take rate.
  3. Turn on a daily early-warning alert for any responder whose score falls 0.3 or more in a week. In a replay it flagged all four 3 to 7 days before they locked out.
