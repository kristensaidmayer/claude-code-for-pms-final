THE FIX: PUT THEM BACK IN THE ROTATION

Three changes, shipped together in one release. This is the only thing I am asking you to approve today.

90sPing wait

Go back from 60 to 90 seconds. At 60s, ping misses spiked and every missed ping was scored like a no.

Vesper and Meteor Mite took 0 of 20 first-in-line pings at 60s, against 79% and 62% at 90s.

SCORERebuilt from the log

Score = share of a responder’s last 30 home-area pings that were taken. A miss counts as not taken, and it can be recovered.

Rebuilt from the ping log every start, so it survives a deploy. On the data we have it puts the four at 0.67 to 0.80. Simulated, not yet live.

ALARMRoster-level

Alert Marcus when the 7-day missed-ping rate tops 5%.

On this data it would have fired on 12 Aug, 5 to 6 days before the weekly report or the support escalation.

What happened

4.2 SHIPPED ON 12 AUG. FOUR HEROES WENT QUIET.

4.2 shortened the ping wait from 90 to 60 seconds and weighted proximity up. A miss costs the same as a turn-down, and scores never recover unless you are pinged and take. So a missed ping at 60s pushed four responders into a loop they could not leave.
