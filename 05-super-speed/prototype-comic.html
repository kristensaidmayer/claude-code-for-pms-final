Mission impact

The problem

4 of 16 heroes quietly fell out of the rotation after the 4.2 update. Callouts nobody took rose from 5.1% to 11.2%.

What this changes

  • Brings back the 90-second wait
  • Rebuilds the routing score from ping history
  • Adds Quiet detection and a check-in

What it's for

A fair way back into the rotation for heroes who were pushed out. Handlers can spot a silent hero in days, not weeks.

If we do nothing

  • Heroes quietly fall out of the rotation
  • Handlers find out weeks later
  • Unanswered callouts stay high
  • Heroes think they've been forgotten

What this solves: responders were unfairly pushed out of the rotation after a software update, and nobody noticed for weeks. This fixes the routing issue behind the lockouts and adds an early-warning signal so handlers can spot silent heroes before they disappear.
