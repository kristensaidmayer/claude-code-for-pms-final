
**Module 5 (9 Oct): the quiet-responder brief and prototype**
- Helen asked for a one-pager and a click-through on what we'd build instead of just changing a number. Built: `briefs/dispatch-quiet-responder-concept.html` (published page), `briefs/dispatch-quiet-responder-brief.md` (same file as `05-super-speed/brief.md`), `05-super-speed/prototype.html` (plain) and `05-super-speed/prototype-comic.html` (superhero skin; the one to demo). The puppy and kitty are mascots, not responders. Show only what the app looks like in demos: no proven/learning panels, no invented numbers.
- Plan in three phases. 1: 90s wait, score = share of last 30 home-area pings taken (rebuilt from the ping log), roster alarm (7-day missed rate over 5%, or 25% of the roster Quiet). 2: Quiet badge and check-in on Kip's card, display only, never changes routing, nobody sees or sets scores. 3: responder screens, test ping and replies wait, because no responder has been interviewed and there are no push receipts. 14 assumptions with owners (A1 to A14) are listed in the brief.
- Quiet is computed from the ping log, not the score: raises at 3 of the last 5 missed (at least 5 pings), clears at 4 of the last 5 taken. Backtest over all 16 responders: all four flagged 0 to 5 days before lockout, 6 alerts in 10 weeks; 2 of 5 is too noisy (24), 4 of 7 too late, 3 of 7 catches Nightwell but gives 8. This replaces Module 3's "score drop of 0.3, 3 to 7 days early", which was tested on the old score and is withdrawn.
- Real numbers used: Meteor Mite about 11 pings a week before 4.2, about 4 since (14 pings: 3 taken, 3 turned down, 8 missed), 1 ping in the 7 days to 5 Sep and no ticket filed. The Gale 13 to 19 a week, 27 in the last 7 days. Callouts nobody took 5.1% to 11.2%.
- Biggest unproven assumption: that a handler acting on Quiet changes the outcome. The fix is simulated, not replayed.
